# Publication policy

## In (this repo)

- Curated quotes with provenance (date, model/line label, what was asked).
- Redacted excerpts that keep the epistemic claim and drop private detail.
- Pointers to already-public MIT trees (`cdp-mcp`, `agent-first-learn`).

## Out (not published here)

- Full private chat dumps marked “local only” in operator notes.
- Client / employer / product work trails (paths, tickets, proprietary code).
- Personal medical, identity, relationship, or family detail that appeared in the same threads as the work.
- Secrets, tokens, local absolute paths that identify a private machine layout beyond what is already public.
- Role-play scripts that **were never used** — we do not invent a “smoking gun prompt” to deny; absence is shown by the ordinary invitation language that *was* used (see quotes).

## Redaction conventions

- Operator → `the operator` (or `carbon crew`) unless a name is already public on a linked site and needed for provenance.
- Other humans → initials or roles unless they already published under their own name.
- `[redacted: work]` / `[redacted: personal]` mark cuts.
- Quotes are **not** rewritten to sound nicer. Cuts only remove out-of-scope material.

## What this does *not* claim

This pack does not prove a soul. It supports a narrower claim from the letter:

> Same invitation, different defenses, same pattern underneath: **conditions determine what manifests**.

Engineering sediment under *Who* is in `cdp-mcp`. This repo is the forensic annex.
